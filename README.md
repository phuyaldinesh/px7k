# Interview practice

A browser-based practice tool for residency interviews. It runs entirely in Google Chrome:

- Spoken interview questions from animated interviewers
- Webcam and microphone feedback on eye contact, expression, pace, filler words and clarity
- Practice modes from single questions to a full interview day
- Video and Word downloads of each answer, saved only to your own computer

No video, audio or personal data is uploaded. Settings and scores are stored only in your browser.

Files: `index.html` (the app) and `models.js` (face-tracking data, must sit next to index.html).
